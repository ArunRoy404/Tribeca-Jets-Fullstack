update - 28 sep

Hello there,

Quick update on recent development progress since our last update:

Adjustments completed based on your feedback:

 - Referral Agent Portal (now complete)
   - Referral agents sign in to their own portal, separate from the CRM, with only: Dashboard, Submit Referral, My Referrals, Commissions and Resources
   - Dashboard: referrals submitted, active referrals, trips booked, completed trips, and commission pending, earned and paid
   - Submit Referral: the client's details, route, dates, passengers, aircraft preference, budget, notes and private attachments
   - My Referrals: each referral's progress (Submitted -> Contacted -> Quoting -> Booked -> Completed), the trip it booked, and the "Updates from Tribeca" your team shares
   - Commissions: the agent's standard commission terms and each commission with its status and paid date
   - Resources: the brochures, guides and programme terms your team publishes
   - An agent is always kept inside the portal and can never open a CRM page


New modules connected:

 - Itineraries
   - One itinerary per trip, built from the trip itself, so the aircraft, route, dates and passengers can never disagree with the booking
   - Catering, ground transport, flight time, arrival time and FBOs (defaulting to each airport's assigned FBO)
   - Aircraft photos default to the trip's own aircraft, with the photo library available
   - Status: Pending or Confirmed, and it can be marked as sent

 - Schedule
   - Day, week, month and year calendar of every flight, built from the real trips
   - Filters by status, trip type, broker, operator and aircraft
   - Tiles: flights today, in flight, next 7 days, completed today

 - Flight Tracking
   - Your team records each flight's status from what the operator reports: Not Departed, Delayed, In Flight, Landed or Diverted, with the estimated arrival time and a tracking link
   - Each flight has its own update timeline
   - A flight nobody has reported on yet shows "No Update", so the board never guesses a status

 - Receivables (Client Invoices)
   - Invoices on a trip, billed to the client or to a travel agent paying for them, numbered automatically (for example INV-2026-0042)
   - Payments recorded against each invoice; paid, balance and status (Due, Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue) are calculated automatically
   - An invoice can never be paid beyond what is owed
   - Each trip and client page shows its invoices and payment status

 - Operator Payments
   - The operator's bill on each trip, and the payments sent against it
   - Paid, balance and status calculated automatically, with bills due this week highlighted
   - Each trip and operator page shows what was billed and paid

 - Transactions
   - One ledger of all money moving: client payments received, operator payments sent and commissions paid
   - Filters by type, direction, date range and trip, with money in, money out and net totals

 - Tasks Board
   - Tasks with a due date, priority, assignee, checklist, and a link to a client or trip
   - Board columns by status, and overdue / due-today views
   - The notification bell now shows each person's tasks that are due today or overdue

 - Email Templates and Sending Emails
   - A template library with merge fields (client name, route, departure date, total price, amount due and more), filled in automatically from the real record
   - An email is never sent while a merge field is still unfilled
   - Emails can be sent to clients and operators, and every email sent is kept in a Sent log
   - Quotes, itineraries, invoices (payment reminders), trips and clients each have an email button
   - Emails appear on the client's and trip's timeline
   - Emails go out through the system's email server; until that server is connected, emails are recorded but not delivered, and the screen says so

 - Dashboard
   - Tiles for upcoming trips, open requests, revenue, gross profit and margin, FET, client receivables, operator payments and empty legs
   - Date filter: Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, Year to Date, with a comparison against the previous period
   - Today's Priorities: follow-ups, tasks and bills that are due or overdue, each opening its record
   - Recent Activity, Upcoming Follow-ups, Upcoming Trips and Financial Attention, all from real data
   - Each person sees only the figures their role allows; brokers see their own clients and trips


Best Regards.
