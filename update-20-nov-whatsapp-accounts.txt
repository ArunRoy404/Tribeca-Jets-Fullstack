WhatsApp assistant - accounts we need from you


Hello Ari,

Right now the WhatsApp assistant runs on our developer accounts, so you
could start using it straight away:

 - The AI (OpenAI) key is our developer's key.

 - The WhatsApp number is Meta's free test number, on our developer's
   Meta account. It can only message 5 pre-approved phone numbers.

Before going live with your team and real clients, it should run on
your own accounts. Here is what we need.



1. OPENAI (THE AI)

What:

 - An OpenAI account with a payment card.

 - One API key.

Where:

 1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign up (or log in).

 2. Settings > Billing: add a payment card.
    You pay only for what the bot uses.

 3. Settings > API keys > "Create new secret key". Copy it.

Then:

 - Paste the key yourself in Settings > WhatsApp & AI > OpenAI,
   and click "Test connection".

 - Please do not send the key by email or chat.



2. META (WHATSAPP)

What:

 - A Meta Business account for Tribeca Jets.

 - A phone number for the business that is NOT already used on the
   WhatsApp app (a new number is easiest).

 - A payment card in WhatsApp (Meta charges per conversation after a
   free allowance).

Where:

 1. Go to business.facebook.com and create a business account for
    Tribeca Jets (if you do not have one).

 2. Settings > Users > People: add our developer as an admin.
    We then set up the WhatsApp connection inside your account for you.

 3. Have the business phone number ready to receive a code by SMS or
    call during setup.

Then:

 - We put the WhatsApp details into your Settings page.

 - With your own number, the 5-phone test limit goes away.



3. ONE QUESTION FOR YOU

To answer a question, the assistant sends the related CRM details
(names, trips, figures) to OpenAI.

 - OpenAI does not train its AI on this data.

 - The assistant asks OpenAI not to save the conversations.

 - Passports and ID documents are never sent.

Are you happy for real client data to be used this way?
We need your OK before the CRM holds real clients.
